Understanding MoE-Driven Collective Traffic in Distributed Training

Networking for AI

Question. How do MoE execution phases and cross-rank collective timing relate to the bursts and gaps observed in distributed-training network traffic?

Approach. I instrument DeepSpeed operations and align execution traces with NIC measurements to characterize collective entry/exit timing skew and network activity across MoE configurations and repeated workload runs. The work is ongoing; the eventual goal is to inform proactive network-control mechanisms.

Preserving Feature Dependencies in Synthetic Tabular Data

AI

Question. Can a generative model better preserve relationships among features rather than only matching individual feature distributions?

Approach. I developed BN-CTGAN by incorporating Bayesian-network structures and conditional probability tables into CTGAN-based synthetic data generation. On 17 evaluated parent–child feature pairs, it reduced mean PMI error by 91.96% relative to CTGAN, improving the preservation of modeled feature dependencies.

Bayesian Networks · Generative Modeling · Tabular Data

Load-Aware Multipath Forwarding in Distributed Networks

Networking

Question. Can a forwarding algorithm reduce transfer delays when traffic contention and link conditions vary across paths in a distributed network?

Approach. I designed and implemented PFS-DFF, adapting proportional-fair scheduling to distribute data fragments across multiple paths based on link throughput and load. On a distributed Raspberry Pi testbed, it reduced end-to-end data transfer time by approximately 9% versus a single-path baseline under high network load.

Network Control · Proportional-Fair Scheduling · Multipath Testbed
2023 · HUFS Capstone Design · Best Paper Award, NCAEIC 2023

Comparing Generative Models for Sensitive Tabular Data Synthesis

AI

Question. Which generative model can most faithfully reproduce the statistical properties of sensitive transportation-worker records while reducing reliance on direct access to the original data?

Approach. I prepared a 10,000-record tabular dataset and compared GAN, CTGAN, and VAE using distributional similarity metrics for individual columns and column-pair relationships. CTGAN achieved the highest overall quality score (91.91%) among the three evaluated models. This fidelity evaluation does not establish a formal privacy guarantee.

Synthetic Tabular Data · GAN / CTGAN / VAE · Distributional Fidelity
2022–2023 · Undergraduate research · HUFS

LSTM-Based Traffic Forecasting in Distributed Networks

Networking

Question. Can temporal patterns in past per-node traffic measurements help forecast future network traffic demand?

Approach. I built a distributed Raspberry Pi network testbed, collected traffic traces, and trained per-node LSTM forecasting models. Across three nodes, evaluation yielded test-set R² values of 0.94–0.96.
