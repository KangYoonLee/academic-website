Research Statement

From the problem I want to solve to the measurements informing my next research steps.

Vision

Make AI experimentation faster and more accessible, without requiring ever-larger computing resources.

I want progress in AI to be constrained more by ideas than by the time and infrastructure required to test them.

Problem

Distributed AI training generates uneven, bursty communication that can leave network capacity poorly utilized.

In workloads such as mixture-of-experts (MoE), collective operations and compute phases interact across ranks. Network-level traffic measurements alone do not fully explain how these execution patterns translate into bursts, gaps, and contention.

Mission

Understand communication demand at its source, then use that understanding to make better network-control decisions.

My research direction is to connect AI workload execution with observed network behavior and, ultimately, design proactive routing, load-balancing, and scheduling mechanisms that reduce communication bottlenecks and training job completion time.

Current Research Focus

MoE execution, collective timing, and observed NIC traffic.

Currently, I instrument DeepSpeed MoE operations and align execution traces with NIC measurements. I am characterizing cross-rank collective entry/exit timing skew and traffic gaps across MoE configurations and repeated workload runs. This measurement work is a foundation for future workload-aware network control.
